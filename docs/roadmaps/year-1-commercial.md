# Year-1 commercial milestones

Three revenue engines: **freemium SaaS** (distribution), **Enterprise Booking Engine** (revenue),
**booking infrastructure** (scale, later). Product work for enterprise happens only when a qualified buyer validates it.

| Months | Focus | Evidence sought | Product implications |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | MVP & pilot; interview potential enterprise buyers | Repeated pain points, workflow fit | MVP roadmap only |
| 3–4 | Launch freemium; 20 qualified enterprise prospects | Active use, credible pipeline | P1 items backed by pilot evidence; billing |
| 5–6 | Offer a paid enterprise pilot | Willingness to pay, implementation scope | Scope reusable enterprise features (custom domain, staff roles) |
| 7–9 | Close first enterprise contract | Repeatable onboarding, recurring revenue | Build only what the contract needs *and* is reusable |
| 10–12 | Retain SaaS users; next enterprise opportunities | Retention, references, pipeline | — |

## Guardrails (blueprint §14.3)

Keep core workflows standardised · prioritise reusable enterprise features · scope and price custom integrations separately ·
no bespoke work without costing maintenance · evidence from qualified buyers before multi-operator, reseller or advanced API work.
